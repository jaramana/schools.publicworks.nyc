Reading these values. Each figure shows the school year it describes. A comparison such as “5.7 points below similar schools” uses the figure the City publishes for that school, not a calculation by this site. The small bars beside a figure count schools of the same type by their published value that year; this site draws them and adds no score. Withheld means too few students were in the group to publish safely, which is not a zero. Not reported means the source published nothing. A measure that does not apply to this type of school is left out rather than shown as missing. See Data.
