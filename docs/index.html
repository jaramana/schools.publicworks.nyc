New York City public school statistics, in one place

The figures New York City publishes about its schools are spread across a quality report, an enrolment snapshot and three admissions directories. This site puts them on one page per school, with the definition, the reporting period and the source next to every number.

This site does not rank schools. There is no overall score, no weighting and no best-school ordering, because the published data does not support one and a family's needs are not a formula.
