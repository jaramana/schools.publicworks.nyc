Every New York City public school, one page each

The figures the City publishes about its schools are spread across a quality report, an enrollment snapshot and three admissions directories. Here they are on one page per school, each number with its definition, its reporting period and its source.

No ranking, no overall score, no recommendation. Every figure comes with the year it describes and where it came from. How to read them.

This is an experimental project, not an official one. It is an independent compilation of data New York City publishes, built to see whether those figures can be made readable in one place. It has no standing, it can be wrong, and it is not checked by anyone at the Department of Education. Before you act on anything here, confirm it against MySchools, the school's own website, or the source linked beside the figure.
