The files behind this site and how they are made.

Downloads

Columns

Join on dbn. An empty value is never a zero, and the status column says why it is empty. Percentages are proportions from 0 to 1. The CSV archive has every row. The workbook's history sheet has a headline set, because Excel stops near one million rows.

DBN
District, borough and school number, such as 02M475.
Comparison group
Schools the City finds similar for one measure. The City publishes the group's average, not its members.
Score out of 5
The City's rating of one measure against the comparison group.
Withheld
Calculated but not published, to protect a small group of students.
Economic Need Index
The City's estimate of the share of students facing economic hardship, from benefit and housing records.
Screened, zoned, open
Admissions methods, set per program. Screened uses grades or other criteria. Zoned favors students in the zone. Open has no academic requirement.

Free to reuse with attribution. Carry the reporting period when you republish a figure. The JSON files the pages read are open too, with no key, and their paths are stable. data/metrics.json lists every measure and its unit, and data/sources.json lists every source, period and limit.

Sources

Process

Each update runs six steps.

  1. Collect

    Every morning the site downloads seven public sources from New York City Public Schools, NYC Open Data and the City's geocoder. The reporting period is read from inside each file.

  2. Match

    Every row is matched to a school by its DBN, the ID every City school has. Never by name, because each file spells names differently.

  3. Check

    Each figure keeps its year, unit and status. A figure the source did not publish stays blank. The build stops if a source changes shape or loses schools, rows or locations.

  4. Band

    For about one figure in six, the City publishes a score from 1 to 5 against a group of similar schools. The site colors four bands from that score, 4 and up, 3 to 4, 2 to 3 and under 2, with the score inside. No score, no color. That grouping is the site's only judgment.

  5. Place

    Each school gets a point from the City's school point file, then the high school directory, then the geocoder. A school none of them has stays off the map.

  6. Publish

    The same checked tables write every page and every download, so they cannot disagree. A late source puts a warning on every page.

Figures

  • A band describes one measure in one year. It is not a rating of the school, and the site has no overall score or ranking.
  • Every figure in a measure comes from the same year. A student group with no figure that year shows as not reported.
  • A blank is never a zero. Not reported means the source published nothing. Withheld means the group is too small to publish. Does not apply means the measure is left off.
  • A school is open if it is in the latest enrollment snapshot or a current directory. No source publishes a status, so a new school can be labeled wrongly.
  • Admissions figures are per program and describe the Fall 2025 season only.
  • Schools report in different years, and each comparison row shows its year.
  • 2019-20 and 2020-21 reflect the pandemic, when testing was canceled or thin.
  • Some measures changed definition in 2018-19 and appear as separate series.
  • A school with grades 6 to 12 files two reports, shown as two rows.
  • A percentage of 12 students is less stable than one of 900. Group sizes are shown where published.
  • Sorting the comparison table orders one measure. It is not a ranking.

Limits

  • The site has no overall scores, ranks or recommendations.
  • It has no address search, school zones or commute times.
  • Survey results, SHSAT figures and specialized high school admissions are not included.
  • Charter schools outside the City's own reporting are not included.
