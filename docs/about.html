About

Schools Finder brings published New York City school statistics into one profile per school, with each figure's definition and reporting period.

Why

The City publishes detailed school data across several separate files. The difficulty is that they are split across a quality report dataset, an enrollment workbook, three admissions directories, and a set of files whose locations change each year. The formats assume you already know what a DBN is and which report type a school files.

This site combines those sources and shows the definition and the reporting period next to every value.

Scope

The site shows published school statistics and their reporting periods. It does not calculate an overall school score.

For applications, use MySchools, the City's admissions system. This site does not determine eligibility or make offers.

Built

Python, pandas, requests, openpyxl and XlsxWriter prepare and validate the data. The website uses static HTML, CSS and JavaScript, served from GitHub Pages, with MapLibre GL for maps.

Independence

No agency reviewed this site.

Credits

Data published by New York City Public Schools and NYC Open Data. Claude was used in development.

Maps: school locations from the NYC Public Schools school point file, and from the Department of City Planning geocoder where the file has no point. District outlines from the Department of City Planning. Basemap from OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles, with data from © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Language

English only, for now. The City publishes in nine languages and MySchools is translated. This site has not been translated or reviewed for other languages.

Reuse

Code is BSD 3-Clause licensed. Compiled data can be reused with attribution; the source data retain their own terms. Carry the reporting period when republishing a figure.
