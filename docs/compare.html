Compare schools

A shortlist of up to twelve schools. Add the measures you care about and they become rows. Every row states the school year it describes, nothing is added up, and no school is marked as better than another.

Where a comparison breaks down. Two schools often last reported a measure in different years, and the period column says so when they did. Schools of different types report different things, so most rows will be blank for one of them. A blank is a gap in reporting, not a low value. More on comparability.
